## ?? Purpose

### SalesInvoiceDTO
Used to transfer sales invoice data between different layers of the application:
- Generate sales invoices from order data
- Process invoice generation in queue services
- Support business rules that create sales invoices

### PurchaseInvoiceModelDTO
Represents a complete purchase invoice document from a supplier:
- Register supplier invoices in accounting
- Update inventory with purchased goods and costs
- Track accounts payable and payment due dates
- Maintain audit trail for tax compliance
- Link purchases to specific orders
